What makes a strong Sales Manager?

A professional Sales Manager does not only chase sales numbers. The manager connects business strategy, people, market execution and performance management.

1. People Build capability through coaching and clear expectations.
2. Numbers Understand target, achievement, gap, run rate and forecast.
3. Execution Convert strategy into daily field actions.
4. Accountability Set owners, deadlines and follow-up routines.
